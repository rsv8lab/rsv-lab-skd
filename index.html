Our Research

Designing synthetic microbial communities for efficient degradation of pollutants.

Phytoremediation

Using hyperaccumulator plants to extract heavy metals from contaminated soil.

Environmental Monitoring

Sensor networks and bioindicators for real-time pollution assessment.
